Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the sign language translation?
+

SIGNAI currently boasts a 95.8% accuracy rate for standard sign language gestures. Our AI continuously learns and improves with more usage.

Which video conferencing tools are supported?
+

We currently support Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams via our Chrome Extension and dedicated desktop apps.

Which sign languages are currently supported?
+

We currently support Korean Sign Language (KSL). We are actively working on adding more languages soon.

Is my video data stored?
+

No. Privacy is our top priority. Video input is processed in real-time and is never stored on our servers.
